# Equivariant E/F/S MVE — serial CPU and single-GPU contract

Decision: one strict serial `trainer: mve` on RACE; no compatibility with scalar 8x0e/29x0e heads or prior checkpoints. `mve.enabled: true`, `mve.version: 1`, exact `output_channels: "2x0e+3x1o+6x2e"`, `regress_forces: "auto"`, explicit `device: cpu` or `device: cuda`, `NN.ema: false`, and positive E/F/S `mve.floors` in units `{"energy":"eV^2","forces":"(eV/Å)^2","stress":"(eV/Å^3)^2"}` are mandatory. CUDA training requires exactly one visible/available GPU and fails without one; never silently falls back to CPU. DeNS+MVE, GPU parallel/DDP, and CuEquivariance/OpenEquivariance backends remain unsupported. Optional normalization/beta/adaptive epsilon/mask and strict restart follow the serial contracts below. No default pretrained checkpoint or training-data dependency is implied by prediction.

RACE predicts graph energy and autograd forces/stress means. ASE stress order is `(xx, yy, zz, yz, xz, xy)` in eV/Å³. The energy head's second scalar is a per-atom log variance; its graph mean plus `alpha * log(n_atoms)` is clamped to `±mve.max_logvar` (default 8) and exponentiated once, so `energy_var = n**alpha * exp(mean logvar)`. `mve.energy_size_exponent` defaults to `{"init": 1.0, "learn": true}` (independent per-atom error prior, learned; ported from the Hawk `mp_vars-eq` trainer, where a size-independent head collapsed on mixed-size batches), and `{"init": 0, "learn": false}` restores the size-independent head. `max_logvar`, `logvar_n_alpha_init` and `logvar_n_alpha_learn` outside `mve` are rejected as aliases; force factors are three 1o vectors per layer/atom, and stress factors are six `(0e + 2e)` vectors per layer/atom, graph-mean-pooled before forming Gram matrices. Public `energy_var[G]`, `forces_var[A,3,3]`, `stress_var[G,6,6]` are **raw** variance/covariance, without a model-side floor. Stress covariance is in the orthonormal e3nn `(0e,2e)` irrep basis, not ASE Voigt coordinates. Hidden irreps must contain 1o and 2e.

`assemble_effective_covariances` validates finite raw shapes, symmetry/PSD and exact units, then adds the configured strictly positive E scalar, F `I3`, and S irrep `I6` floors exactly once in train, validation and export. In irrep coordinates, `Q` maps the above Voigt order to `(0e,2e)`, `Q.T @ Q = diag(1,1,1,2,2,2)`. Stress residual is `Q @ (target-prediction)`. Each eligible E/F/S observation contributes `0.5*(r.T @ Sigma^-1 @ r + logdet(Sigma) + d*log(2*pi))` for d=1/3/6, averaged over graphs/eligible atoms/eligible graphs respectively, never components. Exported Voigt covariance is derived as `Q^-1 @ Sigma_irrep @ Q^-T`; do not add a second Voigt floor. Under physical rotation, force covariance transforms `R C R.T` and irrep stress covariance transforms `D(R) C D(R).T`.

Eligibility is read from **original** ASE calculator results, or a `virial` in original calculator results/`Atoms.info`, before filling missing labels with zeros. Observed zero is eligible; missing E/F/S is not. An observed stress or virial requires a positive finite **original** cell volume; the stress target is ASE Voigt stress or `-virial / original_volume` (symmetric off-diagonals are averaged for 3×3 virials). Nonfinite observed E/F/S is rejected before optimization. For unlabeled molecular frames without a usable cell, a synthetic 30 Å box is only neighbor-list geometry: it is **not** a physical stress volume. Predictions on those frames set `stress_geometry_valid=false` and export `null` for stress mean/raw/effective/derived covariance; energy and force predictions remain available. At least one energy-labelled training frame is needed for atomic references, and each train/validation epoch needs at least one eligible E/F/S observation; a label-free batch contributes only task-local zeros. An absent task contributes no loss/gradient, its count is zero and its reported mean is `null` (including entirely E/F-only splits). Reference fitting uses only eligible training frames. New-run `NN.fname_pkl` must not exist; the trainer refuses to overwrite an existing output. The checkpoint stores safe tensors and plain Python metadata including the v2 manifest, saved canonical config, model/optimizer/controller state and train-only atomic references. Loading uses PyTorch `weights_only=True` without unsafe fallback; malformed, executable-pickle and legacy manifests are rejected before reconstruction. Evaluator deep-copies saved config and applies only prediction-file/output overrides at runtime. It exports raw/effective covariances, derived effective Voigt stress covariance when the original cell is valid, basis `Q`, floor/units, policy and config digest. Non-MVE evaluator mean export remains unchanged.

Two exclusive source modes are supported. `fname_traj` with `ntrain`/`nvalid` uses the fixed-seed disjoint split of one file. Alternatively use **both** distinct `fname_train` and `fname_valid` with exact `ntrain`/`nvalid` frame counts; separate sources retain their source-local frame IDs (starting at zero), reject overlapping geometries and never randomize or concatenate. Fit references and scales from train only. Prediction requires only the saved checkpoint and ASE-readable prediction file: `python evaluate.py model.pkl prediction.xyz --output mve_predictions.json`. The only accepted runtime `predict` keys are `model`, `fname_traj`, and `uncertainty_output`; all frames are processed. CPU prediction of a checkpoint saved with canonical CUDA config reconstructs on CPU without changing that config or its digest and never needs training data. Run focused checks with `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_equivariant_mve*.py'` on the existing Hawk interpreter using `-S`, explicit checkout/site-packages `PYTHONPATH`, one thread, no bytecode writes and empty CUDA visibility for CPU tests only. A single bounded Slurm GPU smoke checks real CUDA separately; neither demonstrates DDP, full training, calibration or publication.

## Phase 2 optional serial training controls

All optional keys live **only** under `mve`, without aliases. Omit them for Phase 1 behavior. `target_norm: {"enabled": true}` fits positive RMS scales from original eligible training labels (E per atom after train-only atomic references, F in eV/Å and S in the orthonormal irrep basis in eV/Å³). Disabled normalization leaves physical Gaussian training and validation NLL unchanged. Enabled normalization divides training residual and effective covariance by the train scale (E by `N * scale_E`), so the training NLL differs from physical NLL only by its data Jacobian/log-determinant offset; validation **always** reports raw unweighted physical Gaussian NLL. Neither mode changes model physical predictions. Train-only fitting records ordered immutable frame IDs and SHA-256 digests of original positions, species, cell, **PBC flags**, and observed E/F/S labels; validation labels are excluded. `target_norm.external` accepts a JSON stats object with exact `bam_torch.mve_train_stats.v1` schema, units, references, positive scales, source/split digests and species; every field must match a recomputation from the current training split. No supplied validation labels or old reference files are trusted.

`beta: 0` disables weighting. A positive finite beta applies only to training: per eligible E/F/S observation use detached `u=clamp(scale,min=1e-12)^beta` divided by that task's serial mean within the selected **optimizer-step batch**. Scale is effective E variance / N², F covariance trace/3 or irrep S trace/6. One optimizer step runs per eligible training batch, without retaining prior batches' graphs; an entirely unlabelled batch is skipped. Epoch metrics accumulate detached per-task numerators/counts over all batches, not means of batch means. This is world-size-one only; no distributed/stream implementation is claimed.

`epsilon: {"enabled": true, "c": {"energy": positive, "forces": positive, "stress": positive}, "min": {…positive values…}}` starts from `min` and adds a positive adaptive floor to the mandatory fixed floor **once** through `assemble_effective_covariances`. E adds `epsilon_E (N scale_E)²`, F/S add `epsilon_(F/S) scale_(F/S)² I` in Cartesian/irrep coordinates. Tracking MSE is **data-scale whitened**, not predicted-covariance whitened: mean `(r_E/(N σ_E))²`, `(r_F/σ_F)²`, or `(Q r_S/σ_S)²` per eligible component (1/3/6). When target normalization is disabled, tracking uses σ=1 for each task but **retains the E per-atom N divisor**; floors retain their fitted train-only scales. After full validation on the **measured** floors, the next epsilon for each eligible task is exactly `max(min, c * tracked_MSE_valid)`; a zero-count task keeps its previous epsilon and `null` metric. The checkpoint contains the best-residual model and its corresponding measured-validation epsilon and measured step, plus the latest training state, latest measured epsilon, next-training epsilon and latest validation step for restart. The prediction exporter always pairs **best parameters with their own measured-validation** epsilon, never the later next-training epsilon, and declares this policy and the training provenance. Best-parameter selection and the ReduceLROnPlateau scheduler use the lambda-weighted **data-scale-whitened** residual MSE, i.e. the same per-component quantities as epsilon tracking (E per atom over σ_E, F over σ_F, irrep S over σ_S, with σ=1 when normalization is disabled), so total-energy eV² of large cells cannot dominate; it is independent of predicted variance and floors. Physical MSE is still reported as `physical_mse`. The checkpoint manifest records this policy as `residual_selection`; checkpoints without it (physical-MSE selection) are rejected. Configure `controller: {"factor": 0.5, "patience": 1, "min_lr": 1e-8}` explicitly to change serial LR behavior; these are structural examples, not recommended production settings.

`mask: {"enabled": true, "max_gpa": 50}` excludes an originally observed ASE Voigt stress only when its largest absolute component exceeds **50 GPa**, converted via `1 eV/Å³ = 160.21766208 GPa`. Original stress eligibility and post-mask stress counts are separate; the frame and its E/F observations remain. All-masked S has count zero and a `null` loss, not an invented zero. Omitting mask leaves observed stress untouched. A saved checkpoint uses `bam_torch.equivariant_mve.v2`, safe `weights_only=True` tensor/plain-value serialization, and policy `raw-best`. Save state includes the best model, latest model/optimizer/LR scheduler, last epoch, best residual-selection metric, best measured/latest measured/next epsilon, validation steps, train stats and torch/NumPy RNG; CUDA training additionally saves/restores the allocated device's CUDA RNG. `weights_only=True` loads CUDA checkpoint tensors onto CPU for CPU-only prediction. Each completed epoch updates the **same newly created output path** belonging to that run. To continue a tiny interrupted run, provide `MVETrainer(canonical_config_with_larger_total_nepoch_and_new_NN_fname_pkl, restart_from="old.pt")`; old input and new output paths must differ, and all physical/model/stats settings and training split/source digest must match. Preexisting output files are never overwritten. No real experiment training, calibration, DDP or Phase 3 stream proof is implied.
